# Arcflight Glossary

This is the canonical glossary for Arcflight terms.

## Aetherite

Black oil-slick magical material used in voidfaring technology and Arkflight systems.

## Arkengine

The magical engine that allows an Arcflight ship to travel through the void. Arkengines are installed ship components and may be fueled by spell ranks, port services, or emergency sacrifice depending on design.

## Arkflight

The setting/system term for magical voidfaring travel by ship.

## AP

Action Points for ship-scale actions in combat or structured ship procedures.

## RAP

Reaction Action Points for ship-scale reactions or response actions.

## Hull

The base ship platform. Hull data defines the vessel's foundational capacity, defenses, expansion slots, movement profile, crew requirements, mounts, and compatibility.

## Derived state

Calculated ship state produced from hull base data plus installed components.

## Current state

Mutable play state such as current hull integrity, strain, lifeveil, morale, supplies, conditions, cargo, and active damage.

## Lifeveil

Arcflight ship resource related to survivability, atmosphere, and magical life-support boundary.

## Levstone

Magical lift/float stone used in ship systems, docks, elevators, and setting infrastructure.

## Pressure

Travel v2 danger/escalation state. Pressure drives hazards, consequence load, and round/event tension.

## Hazard

Travel v2 threat or complication that may be drawn, held, revealed, activated, progressed, or cleared through explicit GM flows.

## Station

A player/crew role or action point in Travel v2, such as Navigator, Engineer, Veilwarden, Watchmaster, or Captain.

## Event Approach

The party's selected method for handling a Travel v2 event. Event approach state can contribute to outcome and tally records.

## Strain

Ship stress resource used for overcharge, hard burn, damage, pressure, or other system costs.

## Travel v2

Arcflight's current GM-directed travel event procedure.

## Player-safe projection

A sanitized state object intended for players. It must remove GM-only, secret, audit, and internal mutation data.

## GM Apply

An explicit GM-confirmed path that turns review/session-local state into a real applied change.
